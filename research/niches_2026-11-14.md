## How to verify a niche (about 10 minutes each)
1. `python3 scripts/research_niches.py worksheet "<keyword>"` creates `research/competitors/<keyword>.csv`.
2. Search the keyword on amazon.com (Books). For the first 10 organic results fill: title, author, ASIN, Best Sellers Rank, number of ratings, stars, paperback price, pages, publication date, series yes/no, notes.
3. Re-run `rank`. GO = at least 3 of the top 10 sell (BSR <= 150,000) with <= 150 reviews or published in the last 18 months.

Worksheet columns: `keyword, position, title, author, asin, bsr, reviews, rating, price, pages, pub_date, series, notes`
