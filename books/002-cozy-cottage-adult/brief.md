# Book 2 brief: Cozy Cottage Coloring Book for Adults

- **Imprint / author:** Little Fox Paper Co / Wren Ashby (same pen name as book 1, the agreed default; revisit if the owner wants a second name for adult books)
- **Audience:** adults (and teens) who like cozy, homey, nature-themed coloring. Not educational, no health or wellness claims (no "stress relief", "therapy", "anxiety").
- **Format:** paperback, 8.5x11, black-and-white interior, **40 single-sided pictures**, blank back of each page, plus title, copyright and "belongs to" pages. Expected 84 pages (4 front + 40 x 2), which is above KDP's spine-text minimum (80+), so the spine carries the title and author.
- **Style:** fine clean line art with plenty of detail (patterns, textures, plants), uniform medium line weight, closed shapes with clearly separated areas, pure white background, no shading/gray/solid black fills/text/borders, composition fills the page with a small white margin. Detailed but not so tiny that it can't be colored with pencils.
- **Theme:** cottage exteriors, gardens, kitchens, reading nooks, cozy animals, tea and baking, seasons of a country garden. Year-round, not tied to a holiday.
- **Banned:** copyrighted characters, films or brands (no named animation studios or fantasy franchises), brand names, celebrity likenesses, famous-author names, any educational or wellness/medical claims, songs/audio/story positioning.
- **Why this niche:** "cozy" and "cottage" themes are a popular aesthetic, but I could not measure demand or competition from this environment, so treat it as a test **[verify]**. Adult coloring is a crowded category.
- **Success check:** all 40 pages pass QA by eye; interior validates; KDP previewer shows no margin problems; the cover is in the owner-approved fun style.
