# Book 1 brief: Cozy Winter Animals Coloring Book

- **Imprint / author:** Little Fox Paper Co / Wren Ashby
- **Audience:** kids ages 4-8 (and parents buying gifts). Winter / cozy theme, year-round "winter" interest, not tied to one holiday.
- **Format:** paperback, 8.5x11, black-and-white interior, 30 single-sided pictures, blank back of each page, plus title, copyright and "belongs to" pages. Expected 64 pages (4 front + 30 x 2); confirm with the build summary.
- **Style:** very bold uniform outlines, big simple shapes, pure white background, no shading/gray/filled areas/text/borders, every shape closed, subject centered with a white margin. Friendly, rounded, cozy.
- **Banned:** copyrighted characters or films (no snow-queen/frozen-style characters, no red-nosed reindeer, no branded toys), brand names, celebrity likenesses, any educational or "learn English" claims, songs/audio/story positioning.
- **Why this niche:** broad but easy to execute, low risk, good pilot for the pipeline. Whether it sells is the test; do not assume it will.
- **Success check:** all 30 pages pass QA by eye; interior validates; KDP previewer shows no margin/bleed issues.
