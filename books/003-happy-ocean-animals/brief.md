# Book 3 brief: Happy Ocean Animals Coloring Book

- **Imprint / author:** Little Fox Paper Co / Wren Ashby
- **Audience:** young kids about ages 3-6 and the adults who buy gifts for them. Simpler and bolder than book 1 (ages 4-8) so the two titles are distinct. Not educational: no counting, letters, "learn" or animal-facts claims; no songs, audio or story positioning.
- **Format:** paperback, 8.5x11, black-and-white interior, 30 single-sided pictures, blank back of each page, plus title, copyright and "belongs to" pages. Expected 64 pages.
- **Style:** extra simple. One main subject per page, very few details, extra thick uniform outlines, huge open areas, friendly smiles, pure white background, no shading/gray/filled areas/text/borders, every shape closed, centered with a white margin.
- **Theme:** friendly sea creatures and simple seaside things. Year-round.
- **Banned:** copyrighted characters, films, songs or brands (no animated-film fish, no "baby shark"-style references, no theme-park or marine-park names), brand names, celebrity likenesses, any educational claims.
- **Why this niche:** ocean animals are a broad, evergreen kids' topic, but it is a crowded one and I could not measure demand from this environment, so it is a test **[verify]**.
- **Success check:** all 30 pages pass QA by eye; interior validates; KDP previewer shows no margin problems; the cover is in the owner-approved fun style.
